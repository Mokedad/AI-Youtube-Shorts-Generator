# Shorts Clipping Service — Sales Kit

## What you sell
"I turn your long podcast / interview / webinar into ready-to-post vertical Shorts
with animated captions — for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts."

You deliver: finished 1080x1920 MP4s, a scroll-stopping headline on each clip,
word-by-word captions, plus a suggested title + caption text for posting.

## Fiverr / Upwork gig
**Title:** I will turn your podcast or YouTube video into viral shorts with captions

**Description:**
Posting long videos but nobody's watching? Short clips are how new viewers find you.

Send me your podcast, interview or YouTube video and I'll find the strongest
moments — the hooks, hot takes and stories — and turn them into vertical Shorts
ready for TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Every clip includes:
- Vertical 9:16 HD (1080x1920), speaker kept in frame
- Bold animated word-by-word captions (most people watch on mute)
- A headline hook at the top to stop the scroll
- Clean cuts that never chop a sentence in half

Just send a link or file. Not sure which package? Message me and I'll make you
one free sample clip from your video.

**Tags:** youtube shorts, tiktok clips, podcast clips, reels editing, video captions

## Outreach message (DM or email to small podcasters / YouTubers)
> Hi {name} — I watched your episode "{episode}" and the part where you talk
> about {topic} at {timestamp} would make a great Short. I made it into one for
> you, free: {link}
>
> I turn full episodes into 6–10 captioned Shorts for TikTok/Reels/YouTube.
> If you like this one, I can do your next episode for $45. No pressure either way!

Tip: always send the free sample. It converts far better than a pitch.
Where to find clients: YouTube channels with 1k–50k subscribers that post long
podcasts but no Shorts; Spotify/Apple podcast hosts; LinkedIn coaches with webinars.

## Daily routine (about 1–2 hours)
1. Find 5 creators with long videos and no Shorts.
2. Make 1 free sample clip for each (paste their video into this tool).
3. Send the outreach message.
4. Deliver paid orders.
